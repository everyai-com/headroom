# Headroom privacy policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

Headroom is made by SAPHAARE LABS PRIVATE LIMITED. This policy covers both editions: Headroom from the
Mac App Store ("Headroom: Disk & Files") and the download from this repository.

## The short version

Headroom collects nothing. Your files, their names, what's in them and how you use the app stay on your Mac.
There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What Headroom reads, and where it stays

- **Your files and folders**: names, sizes, dates, kinds, Finder tags, and where a download came from
  (the "where from" note macOS saves on downloads). Used to show your files by kind, search them, find
  duplicates and say what takes the space.
- **Text in your images and PDFs**: read on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework so you can search it.
- **Duplicate fingerprints**: SHA-256 hashes and Vision image prints, worked out on your Mac.
- **Installed apps**: names, sizes, versions and when they were last used, to show what you could remove.

Everything Headroom works out is kept in its own folder on your Mac (in the App Store edition, inside the
app's sandbox container) and is removed when you delete the app and its data. None of it is sent anywhere.

## Network

- The App Store edition makes no network connections at all.
- The download edition can check a release feed once a day for a newer version, if that build has one set.
  The check sends nothing about you or your files; it only downloads a small version file. It never installs
  anything by itself.

## Changes you make

Headroom only moves files to the Trash, or into folders you approve (Tidy), after you confirm. Every change
is listed in Activity, where you can put it back.

## Children

Headroom collects no data from anyone, children included.
